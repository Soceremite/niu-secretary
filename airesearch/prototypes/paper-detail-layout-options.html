Design decision prototype

论文详情页的 5 种排版路线

目标不是做装饰，而是把论文详情页的阅读路径理顺：先看懂论文是什么，再看图和关键总结，最后进入事实证据。下面每个方案都使用同一篇论文内容，便于判断信息架构。

我的初步判断

推荐优先考虑 A 或 B。A 更像一篇可读论文卡片，适合公开浏览；B 更像研究分析工具，适合高频筛论文和查证据。

Option A / 推荐

阅读器型：左图右关键总结，下方证据

把首屏分成清晰阅读路径：标题和作者在上方，主图占左侧视觉中心，右侧只放五条可扫描总结；facts tabs 放到图文摘要之后。

适合：论文详情页作为“读懂一篇论文”的入口 优点：最稳、最不像后台、改造成本中等。缺点：facts 需要下移一屏。

Paper brief · 2026

Watch, Remember, Reason: Human-View Video Understanding with MLLMs

Jiahao Meng, Yue Tan, Qi Xu, Kuan Gao, Weisong Liu xGH

Method

Three abilities define the taxonomy: watching, remembering, reasoning.

Claim

The view unifies sparse evidence, context memory, and grounded outputs.

Benchmark

Evaluation spans task type, supervision format, modality, and capability.

Limitation

Future work must improve scalable memory-aware video intelligence.

Narrative summary

Video understanding is shifting from short clips to longer and more knowledge-intensive scenarios. This paper frames the field through human-view abilities rather than isolated benchmarks.
